Tails Mod Description
=====================

1. Overview
Tails is a customization mod that lets players equip extra cosmetic parts such as tails, ears, wings, and muzzles.
This 1.20.1 port keeps the feel of the original Tails mod while rebuilding it for a modern Forge environment.

2. Main Features
- Tail, ear, wing, and muzzle selection
- Per-part texture selection
- Three-color tint system
- Rotating character preview
- Library save and load support
- Real-time preview while editing
- Multiplayer appearance synchronization

3. Controls
- Default hotkey: U
- Press U to open the Tails editor.
- Choose the body part from the top tabs.
- Select the desired part from the list on the left.
- Change textures in the texture section below.
- Edit colors from the tint panel on the right.
- Press Save to store the current setup and sync it to the server.
- Closing without saving restores the previous setup.

4. Tail Abilities
In this port, every tail except the raccoon tail gives a direct gameplay effect to the player.

- Fluffy Fox Tail: increased movement speed
- Twin Tails: increased movement speed
- Nine Tails: faster movement than the normal fox tail, higher jump than the bunny tail, and attack power similar to the devil tail
- Dragon Tail: increased armor and knockback resistance
- Raccoon Tail: no special effect
- Devil Tail: +3 damage per hit
- Cat Tail: no fall damage
- Bird Tail: slower falling and reduced fall damage
- Shark Tail: faster swimming and refreshed air underwater
- Bunny Tail: increased jump height

5. How Effects Work
- Tail effects only apply while that specific tail is equipped.
- Changing tails removes the previous tail effect and applies the new one.
- For example, if a player switches from the cat tail to the devil tail, fall damage immunity is removed and the attack bonus is applied instead.
- Nine Tails is part of the fox tail family, but it uses its own upgraded stat profile.

6. Notes
- This mod is built for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.
- Some systems may differ slightly from the original mod internally, but the overall customization feel is preserved as closely as possible.
- Tail balance and passive effects may be adjusted later.
